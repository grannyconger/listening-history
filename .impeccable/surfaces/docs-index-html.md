# Listening History site (all routes)

Scope: the whole single-page site (Story, Eras, Rediscover, Binge, Connections, Explore, Patterns, artist and song pages). Mode: experience. Audience: the owner plus friends and family on phones and computers. Owner wants "the story" remembered. Kept rules: plain short wording, no cards or bordered boxes. Loosened: color may come from school colors and album art, a display face is allowed. Avoid: Wrapped gradients, dark black-and-green app look, dashboard stat tiles.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a music yearbook. Each year is a spread with artist portraits and superlatives that are playable songs. It refuses the category default of stat tiles and identical ranked rows.

OWN-WORLD: Bright white yearbook paper, two school colors (navy #1d2a5b fields and headings, gold #f2b632 rules and superlative labels), near-black ink. Collegiate slab display face (Alfa Slab One) for years and headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything else. Strict portrait grids (4:5 photos, name and hours captioned under, no frames). Superlatives: a large square cover, a gold slab label, the song title big.

STORY: A visitor opens the yearbook cover, flips through year spreads, sees who mattered and what won each superlative, and presses play on any of them. Detail pages read like a student's own yearbook page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy masthead band with the site name in slab gold and the tabs in white. Below, the cover: the title in huge navy slab, the lifetime hours as one sentence, and a ribbon of year covers. Each year then opens as a spread: left half is the giant navy year numeral and a 4 by 2 portrait grid of top artists; right half is the superlatives column (Most played, Most on repeat, Album of the year, Best newcomer) split from the left by a thick gold gutter.

FORM: candidate 1 of 7 on the ordered list (the yearbook), chosen as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the dealt Liner Notes. seed:866fca7f337f83e6. Signature interaction: a portrait or superlative cover lifts and its play state shows a gold "Now playing" caption; motion steps, nothing drifts.
